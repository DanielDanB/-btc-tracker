Welcome

This template is a single Framer code component that renders the whole one-page site: header, hero, services, gallery, optional video section, pricing, about, booking and footer. You never edit code – everything is set from the properties panel on the right.

What makes it different

  • Nine colour themes in one click, or full manual control over every colour on the page.
  • Real booking. Connect a Cal.com event and visitors book straight from the page – inline calendar or popup.
  • Video anywhere an image can go, including a video background for the whole page.
  • Animated floating shapes and an optional custom cursor, both following your accent colour.
  • Responsive by component width, not window width – it looks right in every Framer breakpoint.

1 · Getting started

Opening your remix

You received the template as a Framer Remix link. There is no code to copy and no file to install.

  1. Open the remix link. Framer asks where to place the project – choose your workspace.
  2. Click Remix. Framer creates your own copy; the original stays untouched.
  3. Rename the project (top left) to your salon's name.
  4. On the canvas, click the page once. In the layers panel select the component named Ella Hair Salon Page.
  5. The properties panel on the right now lists every setting described in this guide.
Everything lives in one component. You will not find separate layers for the hero, pricing or footer – they are all rendered by the component, which is why one panel controls the whole page.

Publishing

  1. Click Preview (play icon) to test the real page – booking and the contact form only work here and on the published site, not on the canvas.
  2. Open Publish, pick the free .framer.website address or connect your own domain.
  3. Publish again after any change – edits are not live until you do.

Services

Eyebrow, Heading and a list of Cards. Each card has Title, Description, Image, Video and Video poster. Add cards with +, drag to reorder, and set how many fit per row in 🖋️ Appearance → Service columns.

Gallery

Eyebrow, Heading and Items. Each item takes an Image or a Video plus Alt text – images and videos can be mixed in the same grid. Height and columns are in Appearance.

Video section

Off by default; switch it on in 🧩 Sections → Video section. It shows a full-width video with its own Eyebrow, Heading and Description, plus its own Autoplay, Loop and Controls (handy for a salon tour where visitors should press play themselves).

Pricing

Three groups – women, men and special services. Each has a badge, a title and a list of items with Service and Price. The lists have no length limit; the price is free text, so from $35, 35 € or on request all work. Note is the small centred paragraph under the cards.

About

Eyebrow, Heading, Description, a list of Bullet points, and an Image or Video with Alt text. The bullet character is Appearance → Bullet icon.

Contact details and footer

In Contact & booking you set Eyebrow, Heading, Description, and three label/value pairs: Address label + Address, Phone label + Phone, Hours label + Opening hours. The labels are separate fields so you can translate them.

Footer is a single line of text; Show year puts the current year in front of it automatically.

Images and video – practical notes

  • Upload images through the panel; Framer optimises and serves them for you.
  • Suggested sizes: hero ≈ 1200×1000 px, service card ≈ 800×600 px, gallery ≈ 700×700 px, about ≈ 1000×1200 px.
  • Video files: MP4 (H.264) is the safest. Keep them short and under ~10 MB – they are a background element, not a film.
  • A link ending in .mp4 or .webm also works in an image field – the template detects it and renders a video.

4 · Booking with Cal.com

Cal.com is a free scheduling service. Clients pick a slot, both of you get a confirmation and the appointment lands in your calendar. This chapter covers the setup on cal.com first, then connecting it to the template.

Step A · Set up your booking page on cal.com

  1. Create an account at cal.com. Pick a username you are happy to show – it becomes part of every booking link, e.g. cal.com/ella-salon.
  2. Set your availability. Under Availability, create a schedule matching your opening hours (for example Mon–Sat 9:00–19:00) and set the correct time zone. You can add several schedules, e.g. a shorter Saturday.
  3. Create an event type – one per service you want bookable. Under Event types → New set:
    • Title – what the client sees, e.g. “Women's haircut”.
    • URL – the slug, e.g. womens-haircut. Together with your username this is the link you will paste into Framer.
    • Description – shown next to the calendar.
    • Duration – e.g. 60 minutes. You can offer multiple durations.
  4. Set the location in the event's Location field – for a salon choose In person and enter the address, or Attendee address for home visits.
  5. Connect your calendar under Apps → Calendar (Google, Outlook, Apple). Cal.com then blocks slots you are already busy in and writes new bookings into that calendar.
  6. Tune the limits in the event's Limits tab: minimum notice (e.g. 2 hours), buffers before and after, how far ahead people can book, and a daily maximum.
  7. Adjust the questions in Advanced → Booking questions. Name and email are always there; add a phone number or a “which service” dropdown. You can also require confirmation before a booking is final.
  8. Test it – open cal.com/your-name/your-event in a private window and make a booking. Check that the confirmation e-mail arrives and the event appears in your calendar.
Which link do I need? Everything after cal.com/ – that is username/event-slug, for example ella-salon/womens-haircut. Pasting the whole address works too; the template shortens it for you.

Step B · Connect it in Framer

Open Contact & booking. The first field, 📅 Booking, decides what appears at the bottom of the page:

ModeResult
Contact formOnly the contact form (default). Needs Formspree – chapter 5
Cal.com calendarThe booking calendar embedded in the page, no form
Cal.com buttonA button that opens booking in a popup, no form
Form + Cal.com buttonBoth – people choose how to reach you

Then fill in Cal.com link with username/event-slug. Until you do, the section shows a short hint instead of a calendar.

Step D · Test

  1. Open Preview – the calendar does not load on the Framer canvas, because the canvas blocks external scripts.
  2. Pick a slot and complete a test booking.
  3. Publish and repeat once on the live site.
Built-in safety net. If the Cal.com script is blocked (ad blockers, strict privacy settings), the page shows a button linking to your Cal.com page instead, so visitors can always book. The script is only loaded when booking is switched on and a link is filled in – otherwise the site loads nothing external at all.

Good to know

  • The small “Cal.com” credit under the calendar belongs to their free plan and can only be removed in a paid Cal.com plan.
  • One event type per link. To offer several services, either point the button at your main event, or add more buttons through Framer's own layers.
  • Prices, deposits and payments are configured on cal.com, not here.

5 · Contact form

The form posts to Formspree, which forwards submissions to your inbox. No server needed.

  1. Create a free account at formspree.io and confirm your e-mail.
  2. Create a new form and copy its endpoint – it looks like https://formspree.io/f/abcdwxyz.
  3. Paste it into Contact & booking → Formspree endpoint.
  4. Publish, send a test message and confirm the first submission in the e-mail Formspree sends you.
FieldMeaning
Name / Email / Phone / Message placeholderPlaceholder text in the four inputs
Button text / Sending textLabel before and during submission
Email subjectSubject line of the e-mail you receive
Success messageShown after a successful send
Error messageShown if the send fails

Without an endpoint the form politely reports that it is not connected yet, so a visitor never gets a silent failure. If you only take bookings through Cal.com, switch the mode to Cal.com calendar or Cal.com button and the form (and its fields) disappear.

6 · Layout and responsiveness

How the component is sized

The component is set to Fill width and Fit content height: it spans the width of its frame and is exactly as tall as its content. Add a service card or a pricing row and the frame grows by itself – you never drag it to fit.

Breakpoints

Layout changes follow the width of the component, not the width of the browser window. That is why the page looks correct inside a narrow phone breakpoint even while you are editing on a wide canvas.

Component widthWhat changes
up to 992 pxHero becomes one column and centres, service cards and gallery drop to 2 columns, price cards stack
up to 768 pxMenu collapses into the hamburger, hero media and video section get shorter
up to 480 pxEverything is one column, buttons go full width, page padding tightens

The mobile menu

The slide-in menu is built from your Menu items plus the CTA button. It only exists while open, and closes on a link, on the backdrop or with Esc.

Hero height

Appearance → Hero height (vh) is a share of the screen height: 100 means a full-screen hero. It is measured in small-viewport units, so a phone with a dynamic address bar gets the same result as your canvas.

7 · Troubleshooting

The panel does not show a setting described here

Check the very first field, Version. This guide documents v8 · Remix ready. If your remix predates it, ask for the current remix link.

The calendar does not appear

  • Test in Preview or on the published site – the Framer canvas runs in a sandbox that blocks external scripts.
  • Check that Cal.com link is username/event-slug and that the same address opens in a browser.
  • Origin and Embed script must stay empty unless you self-host Cal.com.
  • If a button linking to Cal.com shows up instead of the calendar, the embed was blocked in that browser – usually an ad blocker or strict third-party-cookie settings. Booking still works through the button.

The form does not send

  • Is Formspree endpoint filled in and does it start with https://formspree.io/f/?
  • Did you confirm the first submission in the e-mail Formspree sent you? Until then messages are held.
  • Test on the published site, not on the canvas.

The page looks cut off at the bottom on a phone

The component reports its height correctly, but Framer remembers the height it measured on the canvas. Select the component on the phone breakpoint and set it – and the frame around it – to Fit content rather than a fixed height.

Colours changed but a button did not

Check 🎨 Colors → Buttons use accent. When it is off, buttons take Button and Button hover instead of the accent.

Shapes or the cursor feel distracting

Lower Count, Speed and Opacity, or switch Floating shapes → Enable off. For the cursor use Cursor → Mode → System. Visitors who ask their system to reduce motion automatically get a calmer page.

What cannot be changed from here

  • The “Cal.com” credit under the calendar – a Cal.com plan setting.
  • Formspree's own limits and spam filtering – your Formspree plan.
  • Page title, favicon, social preview, domain and SEO – Framer's own page and site settings.

Appendix · Standalone HTML version

Besides the Framer template there is a single-file HTML build of the same site, for hosting outside Framer. Skip this appendix if you are staying in Framer.

index.html contains the markup, styles and scripts in one file – no build step, no dependencies, no CDN. Upload it anywhere (FTP, Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages) or open it locally by double-clicking.

Where the settings live

At the top of the <script> block there is a SITE object – the equivalent of Framer's panel:

const SITE = {
    theme: "noir",   // noir | midnight | emerald | gold | violet
                     // coral | mono | daylight | custom
    colors: {},      // your own colours when theme = "custom"
    style: {},       // fonts, radii, spacing, media heights
    effects: {},     // headerBlur, heroGlow, hoverLift, pricingGlow
    shapes: {},      // { enabled: false } turns the shapes off
    cursor: {},      // { mode: "Default" } turns the custom cursor off

    booking: { mode: "form", calLink: "" },   // form | inline | popup | both
    form: { formspreeEndpoint: "" },
}

Defaults for every value are in the DEFAULTS object right below – you only override what you want to change. Booking and the form behave exactly as described in chapters 4 and 5.

Texts and images

Texts are edited directly in the HTML. Images are loaded from an assets/ folder next to the file:

assets/hero-salon.jpg
assets/service-1.jpg … service-4.jpg
assets/gallery-1.jpg … gallery-8.jpg
assets/about-salon.jpg

Until those files exist the page shows neutral dark placeholders – nothing breaks. A <video> tag with the same classes can replace any image.
